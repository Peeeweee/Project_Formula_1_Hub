# Learning and records upgrade / 20 October 2026

- Driver championship archive: Jolpica completed-season standings, 1950-2025 (76 seasons, 35 champions). Ongoing seasons are excluded. Duplicated years are ignored and coverage gaps are displayed. Reference: https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/hall-of-fame
- Constructor championship roll: completed titles 1958-2025, 68 seasons, 15 teams. Team names are grouped by championship-winning identity rather than merged predecessor organisations. The bundled roll does not include per-season win or point statistics for teams. Reference: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-the-f1-constructors-championship.66nTfWSqrUYv3bnbosPkHV
- The championship archive does not claim to cover every historical race-wins, poles or lap-record statistic. Driver points and wins shown in details are from title-winning seasons only.
- Standard and Sprint weekend explanations are separate. Reference: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-the-f1-sprint.55yJBEiF7vYkZEwSV9lZJ9
- Points explorer uses full-points Grand Prix and Sprint scales, with teammate examples and the post-2024 removal of the fastest-lap bonus. Reduced-distance rules are explicitly outside the example.
- Circuit artwork is generated from geographic LineStrings in https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits (MIT, Tomislav Bacinger), downloaded 20 October 2026. Each reference-atlas venue is matched by location and asserted to be within 15 km. Projection corrects longitude for latitude before fitting each outline. These are reference layouts, not certified current event maps. Retained specifications may be historical. Attribution/license: public/circuit-data-LICENSE.txt. The source snapshot is artifacts/circuit-source.geojson.
- Backgrounds use small local geometries instead of additional model downloads. One canvas remains across routes and pauses for dialogs, hidden tabs and reduced motion. The intro remains independent.
- Glossary demonstrations use the optimized existing 2022 Mercedes model, plus schematic wings, energy storage, tire surfaces and race-control movements. The car is not presented as a 2026 model. This is an explanation, not a physical or live telemetry simulation. Pause, replay, manual steps and orbit controls are available. Text remains available if 3D fails.
